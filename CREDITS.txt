Orbital Kinetic Strike — Credits

Code, shaders, textures, 3D models, and sounds: created for this mod.

Block, item, particle, and Earth cloud textures: procedurally generated by
tools/generate_textures.py.
Auxiliary textures (Moon, planet atlas, Saturn radial ring profile):
procedurally synthesized client-side by CosmosTextures.java.
3D models (finned/railed accelerator ring module, 4-fin grooved octagonal
tungsten needle): generated by tools/generate_models.py.
Sounds (15 tracks orbital_01_mark.ogg .. orbital_15_hum.ogg): synthesized by
tools/generate_sounds.py from oscillators, filters, harmonic chords, and
convolution reverb. No external samples or recordings.

Public scientific and mathematical references used in code:
- NASA/JPL Solar System Dynamics, "Approximate Positions of the Planets" (E. M. Standish): J2000 Keplerian elements.
- Marius Bjørge (SIGGRAPH 2014): dual-filter bloom pyramid (13-tap downsample / 9-tap tent upsample).
- Krzysztof Narkowicz (2015): analytical ACES filmic tone mapping approximation.
- Kay & Kajiya (1986): ray-box slab intersection.
- Frisvad (2012): singularity-free orthonormal basis construction.

All mod-specific assets (code, shaders, textures, 3D models, and audio) are
original creations made for this project.

Build-time tools used to generate assets (not bundled in the mod):
NumPy, Pillow, python-soundfile / libsndfile (OGG Vorbis encoding).
